# raindrop for DAOs — script

The slow, quiet cut: Raindrop as low-friction, distributed decision making for DAO fund distribution.
Two halves: **the DAO way**, a grey machine that only moves when someone cranks it, and **the Raindrop way**,
a network that grows toward the work. One headline, at most one supporting line, and one visual per slide.
~164 s. Timings match `video.html`.
