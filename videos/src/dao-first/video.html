Every organization has the same problem. A business. A non-profit. A charity.
How do you put the moneywhere it does the most good?
The answer is already in your people. They know who's doing the real work.
The hard part is using what they know.
The DAO approach Put every decision to a vote.
One proposal at a time. Draft. Discuss. Vote. Wait. Execute.
It only moves when someone cranks it. Reminders. Deadlines. Quorum. Most people never vote.
So big decisions rest on a few votes. Lump sums, months apart, while the work moves on.
The Raindrop network Let funding follow trust, continuously.
Everyone identifies those contributing to the mission. The votes are tallied through liquid democracy. No proposals, no deadlines, change yours anytime.
The identified contributors grow in power. Every round, new tokens rain on them: more voting power, more spending power.
Nobody has to manually push it forward. It runs in the background, always up to date.
