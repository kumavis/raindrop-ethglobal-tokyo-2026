The DAO way Built like a machine.
It runs on proposals. A few big asks. Each one all-or-nothing.
Every decision runs the same slow cycle. Draft. Discuss. Vote. Wait. Execute.
Someone has to keep cranking it. Reminders. Deadlines. Quorum.
Then it pays out in lump sums. A handful of votes decide the biggest payouts.
Between cycles, it can't sense anything. The work moves on. The machine doesn't notice.
The Raindrop way Grown like a network.
It starts with one token. Your DAO issues a Raindrop governance token.
Every token carries two rights.
A vote
A claim on the treasury
The treasury is the market. Donations, investment and revenue all flow in as liquidity.
Anyone can sell the token into it, at the market rate.
Voting means endorsing people. Point your weight at contributors you trust. You keep your tokens.
An endorsement says: give them more say. More voting power. More spending power.
Trust flows through the network. Raindrop scores everyone by how the whole network endorses them.
Every round, it rains. New tokens are minted and airdropped by trust score.
Keep them, or sell them.
